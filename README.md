# Island Survival

**Small Survival Game for a Hckclub Jam**

Island Survival is a work-in-progress, top-down pixel-art survival game made with Unity. Explore an island, prepare during the day, and defend yourself against zombies after dark. Each night brings a tougher challenge.

## How to play

1. **Gather supplies.** Chop trees for wood and walk over stones to collect them.
2. **Find food.** Animals drop food that you can eat to restore health.
3. **Light the signal.** Bring 3 wood and 2 stone to the campfire and press **E**. Lighting it restores your health once.
4. **Survive the night.** Zombies appear after dark. Their numbers and strength increase as you survive more nights.

Days and nights each last five minutes. When you die, the game shows how many nights you survived.

## Controls

| Key | Action |
| --- | --- |
| **WASD** or **arrow keys** | Move |
| **R** | Chop a nearby tree |
| **F** | Swing your sword |
| **E** | Light the signal at the campfire |
| **H** | Eat food and heal |
| **M** | Toggle sound |

## Features

- A day-night cycle with restricted visibility after dark
- Increasingly challenging zombie encounters
- Sword combat, health, food, and resource gathering
- Animals, changing weather, and rain ambience
- An in-game objective, sound controls, and a restart screen
