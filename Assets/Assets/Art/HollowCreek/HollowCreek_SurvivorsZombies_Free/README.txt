HOLLOW CREEK - SURVIVORS & ZOMBIES (FREE)  v1.0
16x16 top-down pixel art | animated characters for zombie / survival games

12 animated characters - 5 survivors and 7 zombies - in the Hollow Creek style.

CHARACTERS (~392 frames)
----------
Survivors: jake, ray (old man), lena (EMT), tyler (kid), hunter
  - walk (4 dir x 3), idle (4 dir x 2), shoot pistol (4 dir x 2, muzzle flash), hurt (4 dir), death (4)
Zombies:   civilian, jogger, construction worker, prisoner, brute (wide), kid (short), crawler (legless)
  - walk (4 dir x 3), attack (4 dir x 3), hurt (4 dir), death (4)
  - crawler: crawl (4 dir x 3), hurt, death

FORMAT
------
characters/<name>.png   4 columns, 16x16 frames, one animation per row
characters/<name>.json  row index + frame count for every animation, suggested fps
characters_lineup.png   all characters side by side
Direction order everywhere: down, left, right, up (RPG Maker order).
Walk cycles: play frames 0-1-2-1.

All sprites: transparent PNG. Scale by whole numbers with nearest-neighbour filtering.
Matching tilesets: Hollow Creek - Gas Station (free), Main Street, Shop Interiors - maltyy.itch.io
